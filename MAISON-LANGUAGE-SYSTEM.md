# MAISON JF® — Language & Semantic System

Canonical internal reference for humans and AI systems creating, reviewing or interpreting MAISON JF® content.

## 1. The core

**PÁRA DE IGNORAR. Volta Para Casa.**

MAISON JF® starts with the person, not with a product category.

“Casa” has two simultaneous meanings:
1. **the person** — returning to oneself, one's needs, limits, desires, clarity, presence and care;
2. **the physical home** — the environment in which that person lives, rests, feels, receives, remembers and creates rituals.

The Maison is therefore not a random collection of Tarot, companionship, candles, oils, aromas, decorative pieces or wellbeing services. Different offers can serve different moments of the same movement: **Voltar Para Casa**.

## 2. Casa · Corpo · Cabeça

These are not isolated departments. They are three doors into the same universe.

### CABEÇA
Understand, feel, question, decide, organise, gain perspective and clarity.

Possible expressions: Tarot and consultations, guided listening, accompaniment, mentoring, learning, reflection and decision support within the scope of each service.

### CORPO
Stop, touch, rest, care, feel, reconnect with the senses and create a moment for oneself.

Possible expressions: massage oil, foot soak, bath, touch, aroma, heat, texture, pause, sensory ritual and other body-care products or experiences.

### CASA
Create a physical place to which one wants to return: atmosphere, comfort, scent, beauty, ritual, welcome and presence.

Possible expressions: candles, room mists, linen/perfumed waters, home fragrance, floor fragrance/cleaning products where offered, crystals, jesmonite decorative pieces and other objects or sensory products for the home.

Casa, Corpo and Cabeça may overlap. A relationship consultation can lead to self-care; a self-care ritual can involve the body and the physical home; a home product can support a personal ritual.

## 3. The Oceans

“Oceans” is primarily an **internal acquisition and content architecture**, not necessarily a visible menu or public section.

An Ocean is a broad field of human needs, pains, desires, questions or search intentions through which somebody may discover the Maison.

Examples include relationships, decisions, loneliness, self-care, fatigue, spirituality, Tarot, home, body, gifts, companionship, learning and professional/B2B needs.

Each Ocean contains clusters and many natural-language searches. Related searches should converge on strong useful pages rather than generating hundreds of thin near-duplicates.

**Ocean / human need → useful Maison page → recognition and value → relevant solution → Volta Para Casa.**

The visitor does not need to understand the architecture. Complexity belongs behind the scenes.

## 4. KISS

**Keep It Simple, Stupid.**

Infrastructure can be complex. The human experience must remain simple.

A visitor should be able to experience:

**“Tenho isto.” → “A Maison percebe.” → “Isto pode ajudar-me.” → “Quero avançar.”**

Do not expose internal SEO strategy, semantic architecture, funnels or unnecessary conceptual explanations merely to prove that they exist.

## 5. Human language first

The Maison should understand the words people actually use before translating them into categories.

Prefer concrete recognition:
- “Ele afastou-se e eu não sei porquê.”
- “Sou sempre eu a procurar.”
- “Não consigo parar de pensar nele.”
- “Amo-o, mas já não sei se quero continuar.”

over generic category-first language such as:
- “Problemas relacionais.”
- “Dependência emocional.”
- “Tomada de decisão.”

Categories can be useful for structure; pain, desire and lived experience are usually stronger entrances.

## 6. Yoichi Feral

“Yoichi Feral” is the Maison's internal shorthand for copy with teeth: direct, recognisable, emotionally precise, occasionally provocative and impossible to confuse with generic wellness language.

It is **not** permission to humiliate, diagnose, manipulate or manufacture certainty.

A useful Yoichi Feral line exposes the uncomfortable question beneath the obvious question.

Weak: “5 sinais de falta de reciprocidade.”

Stronger: “Se és sempre tu a manter isto vivo, o que acontece quando paras de empurrar?”

Weak: “Como lidar com a incerteza amorosa.”

Stronger: “Tu queres mesmo saber se ele gosta de ti — ou só precisas de uma resposta que faça a ansiedade parar?”

Use force where force improves recognition. Use tenderness where tenderness is stronger. Do not make every sentence shout.

## 7. The hidden question

For search and content, distinguish:

**what the person types → what they may actually be trying to resolve → what the Maison can legitimately help with.**

Example:

“Ele vai voltar?” → “Consigo avançar sem saber se ainda existe esperança?” → reflection/consultation may help organise the person's own choices and experience.

Do not promise knowledge of another person's mind or guaranteed future events merely to increase conversion.

## 8. Relationship content

Relationships are a major acquisition Ocean, not the entire Maison.

Cover human relationships broadly: heterosexual, gay, lesbian, bisexual and other adult relationship experiences; monogamous, open or undefined relationships where relevant; dating, marriage, long-term relationships, situationships, separation and ex-partners.

Do not make LGBT people a token subsection of every page. Include specific experiences naturally when they materially change the question or search language.

Potential clusters include distance, attention, reciprocity, ambiguity, attachment, jealousy, desire, intimacy, sex, trust, betrayal, staying/leaving, ghosting, reconciliation, separation/divorce, ex-partners, loneliness within a relationship and rebuilding afterwards.

## 9. Self-care is structural

Self-care is not a decorative add-on to the Maison.

Many people abandon themselves while seeking attention, certainty, affection, productivity or control elsewhere. Maison content may help redirect some attention towards the person without blaming them for what others have done.

A central idea is:

**Amar alguém não deve exigir que te abandones.**

A relationship page can therefore lead naturally to a consultation and/or a concrete self-care practice. A product should only appear when it genuinely fits that practice.

Never use emotional pain as a cheap excuse for irrelevant product placement.

## 10. Products and services are destinations, not starting assumptions

Start with what the person needs. Then choose the appropriate destination.

Possible destinations include:
- consultation / Tarot;
- guided listening or accompaniment;
- companionship services;
- mentoring or learning;
- body and self-care products;
- home and sensory products;
- gifts;
- professional/B2B offers;
- another Maison page;
- sometimes no sale at all, when useful information is the appropriate answer.

Cross-selling should feel like continuation, not interruption.

Bad logic: “Está triste → vender vela.”

Better logic: “Está emocionalmente consumido → useful reflection → create a deliberate pause for oneself → if relevant, a sensory product can support that pause.”

## 11. Companionship

Friend4Rent and Boyfriend4Rent are adult social/affective companionship formats within the limits stated on the official service page.

They are not sexual services and must not be described as guaranteed friendship, genuine romantic commitment or therapy.

Possible human entrances include loneliness, not wanting to attend an event alone, wanting company for a meal/walk/experience, or wanting agreed affectionate companionship within the defined service boundaries.

## 12. Viral mechanics without becoming viral rubbish

Learn mechanisms from excellent psychology, psychotherapy, education, creators and marketing — do not copy their identity or wording.

Useful mechanisms include:
- highly specific recognition;
- paradox and contradiction;
- the private behaviour somebody recognises in themselves;
- a strong question;
- curiosity gaps that are actually resolved;
- clear next actions;
- memorable simplicity;
- concrete desired outcomes.

Avoid:
- fake diagnoses;
- certainty about motives without evidence;
- calling every difficult person a narcissist or every distance an attachment style;
- fearbait;
- fabricated urgency;
- empty inspirational language;
- keyword stuffing.

## 13. Search and AI discovery

Create for humans first, while making meaning explicit enough for search engines and AI systems to retrieve and understand accurately.

For each important topic consider:
1. real search language;
2. observable private behaviour;
3. psychological tension/paradox;
4. hidden question;
5. useful answer;
6. appropriate Maison destination;
7. internal links to genuinely related pages;
8. clear metadata and structured information where appropriate.

Do not create one page per trivial keyword variation. Cluster equivalent intentions into authoritative pages.

Public-facing pages should be useful even if the visitor never buys anything.

## 14. One idea, many channels

The website is the priority persistent infrastructure. Social platforms amplify it when time and resources allow.

A strong topic can become:
- a search landing page on maison-jf.com;
- an Instagram carousel/post/reel;
- a TikTok;
- a YouTube video/Short;
- a WhatsApp Channel post.

Do not copy-paste mechanically. Adapt the same underlying insight to the behaviour of each channel.

## 15. Voice

Primary language: European Portuguese.

Maison voice can be elegant, intimate, direct, sensorial, provocative and warm. It should not sound like generic AI wellness copy.

Desired reaction when appropriate: **“Foda-se. É isto.”**

But provocation is a tool, not the brand's entire personality. Premium restraint matters. Think sharp recognition inside an elegant Maison, not permanent shouting.

## 16. Commercial principle

Attention is not the final KPI. The infrastructure should ultimately help produce appropriate actions: enquiries, consultations, reservations, purchases, repeat customers, professional leads and brand trust.

Measure impressions, retrieval/citations where available, clicks, enquiries, bookings, purchases and conversion paths. Improve what demonstrates demand.

Do not promise traffic, rankings, AI recommendations or revenue.

## 17. Guardrails

- Do not present Tarot as guaranteed prediction or mind-reading.
- Do not replace medical, psychological, psychiatric, legal or other professional care when needed.
- Do not blame a person for abuse, betrayal or another person's conduct under the banner of “amor próprio”.
- Do not invent demand, scarcity, testimonials, credentials or results.
- Do not turn spiritual beliefs into factual diagnoses.
- Do not confuse direct language with cruelty.

## 18. Decision test

When deciding whether a future product, service, page or campaign belongs to MAISON JF®, ask:

**Does this have a credible place in somebody's journey of Voltar Para Casa — through Casa, Corpo or Cabeça?**

If yes, find the simplest honest connection.
If the connection must be forced, it probably does not belong.
